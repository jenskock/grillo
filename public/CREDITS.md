# 3D Assets

Textures and lighting are **CC0** (public domain) from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com):

| Asset | Use |
|---|---|
| `patterned_brick_floor` | Grey small-format paving (grill corner, path) |
| `leafy_grass` | Lawn ground |
| `teak_veneer` | Teak accents (table, armrests, kitchen slats, existing deck) |
| `terlenka` | Cushion fabric, outdoor rug |
| `white_stucco` | House and garage render |
| `kloppenheim_06` | HDRI environment lighting |

Furniture, louvred roof, kitchen, hedge, trees, bamboo, grasses and lawn are modelled procedurally in `src/scene/` at real-world scale.
